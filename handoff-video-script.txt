What is your idea? (Less than 50 words)  — 48 words

Handoff turns a Lagos beat handoff into a payment. The producer
opens a session. Everyone sets their share in naira, then signs by
receiving one hundred naira, so the bank confirms the account. The
artist pays once. The stems download and everybody is paid in the
same moment.


Why do you think it's the solution to the problem statement?
(100 words)  — 99 words

Free split sheets exist. MCSN, one of our collecting societies,
publishes one. Almost nobody signs them, and the reason is not
ignorance. The producer wants the document and the artist holds the
money, so the person with less power is asking the person with more
for a favour. That is easy to ignore. Paperwork has never fixed it.
Handoff does not add another document. It changes who is holding
what. The producer holds the stems. The files do not unlock until
the money lands, and the money cannot land except against a signed
split. Nobody has to be persuaded.


================================================================
DO NOT SAY THESE
================================================================

  "We have shipped together."
       Your team is assembled, not proven. Invites
       "what did you ship together?"

  "Only the owner can receive it."
       A ₦100 credit proves control, not ownership.
       Nigerians transact through a sibling's account constantly.

  Anything about Dapper Music, the EFCC, or Samklef.
       Handoff settles the session, not years of catalogue
       accounting. Invites "would this have paid Samklef?"
       — which answers itself. No.

  "Payaza's sandbox FAKES the transfer."
       Say "simulates." Never use the verb of fraud about money
       in front of a chartered accountant who runs a payments
       company.
